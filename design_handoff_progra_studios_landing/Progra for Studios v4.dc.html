How it works

  1. 1
    Create your group. Set it up in a few minutes.
  2. 2
    Invite your members. Everyone in the group is connected, so new members see people they already know and practice with.
  3. 3
    Practice becomes visible. Members log their sessions in and outside class, see friends’ progress, and send encouragement with Cheer or Nudge.
  4. 4
    Spot who’s drifting. See when a member’s activity drops, so you can reach out personally before they cancel.
